01 · CV · Research

OptiSAR

Multi-sensor land-cover segmentation on MultiSenGE. Stacks Sentinel-2 (10 optical) + Sentinel-1 (VV/VH) across 4 dates into a U-TAE (shared spatial encoder, temporal attention, U-Net decoder). Tile-held-out eval on 31UEQ: weighted F1 0.9387 and kappa 0.5757, beating the ConvLSTM baseline. Staged train: freeze encoder, then full fine-tune.
